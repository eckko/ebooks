|| Shri Shiva Tandava Stotram (प्रशंसा का गीत/hymn of praise) ||
From the forest of matted hair (जटा/tangled hair), the flowing water (पवित्र जल/holy water) purifies the neck area where the snake is hanging like a high garland (माला/necklace).
With the damaru emitting the sound (आवाज़/sound) of damat-damat, Shiva did the fierce Tandava dance; may he extend welfare (कल्याण/blessings) to us.
The matted hair holds the agitatedly swirling celestial river (गंगा/Ganga) whose waves are moving restlessly (चंचल लहरें/restless waves) on His glorious forehead.
On the surface of His forehead, the fire is burning brightly (चमकती आग/glowing fire) with dhagat-dhagat, may I have deep love (प्रेम/devotion) every second for Him who has the young moon on His head.
He is the companion of the sports of the daughter of the mountain king (पार्वती/Parvati), and His mind is filled with happiness (आनंद/joy) by the universe.
With a stream of compassionate glances (कृपा की दृष्टि/merciful glances), He blocks uncontrollable misfortunes; may my mind find joy in that supreme entity who wears directions as clothes.
The reddish-brown snakes around His matted hair have glowing hoods (चमकते फन/shining hoods) whose jewel-light colors the faces of the directions like kumkum.
He wears an upper garment (उत्तरीय वस्त्र/shawl) made of the thick skin of an intoxicated elephant; may my mind find wonderful joy in the Lord of all ghosts (भूत-प्रेत/spirits).
His footstool is covered in grey by the dust (फूलों की धूल/pollen) from the flowers on the crowns of all the gods, including Indra.
His matted hair is tied together by the king of serpents (सांपों का राजा/serpent king), and may He who holds the moon on His head bring us long-lasting prosperity (लक्ष्मी और समृद्धि/wealth and welfare).
On the altar of His forehead, the sparks of fire (चिंगारियां/sparks) destroyed the god of love (कामदेव/Cupid), and the leader of the gods bows to Him.
He is adorned with the crescent moon on His head; may that bearer of skulls give us the ultimate wealth (महान संपत्ति/great spiritual wealth).
His wide forehead is burning with dhagat-dhagat fire, which completely subdued (वश में किया/neutralized) the fierce five-arrowed Cupid.
He is the sole artist (कलाकार/creator) who draws decorative lines on the breasts of Parvati; my deep love is in that three-eyed Lord (शिवजी/Lord Shiva).
His neck is dark like the dense midnight of a new moon night, held back by a gathering of fresh clouds (बादल/clouds).
He holds the Ganga, wears an elephant skin, is beautiful with the crescent moon, and may that supporter of the universe expand our prosperity (भाग्य और वैभव/good fortune).
His neck resembles the beauty of the dark expansion of a fully bloomed blue lotus (नीला कमल/blue lotus).
Remember the destroyer of Cupid (कामदेव को नष्ट करने वाले), the destroyer of Tripurasura (त्रिपुरासुर का नाश करने वाले), the destroyer of worldly bonds (संसार बंधन काटने वाले), the destroyer of Daksha's sacrifice (यज्ञ का विनाश करने वाले), the destroyer of Gajasura (गजासुर का वध करने वाले), the destroyer of Andhakasura (अंधकासुर का संहार करने वाले), that destroyer of Death (यमराज के भी नाशक/Yamraj's destroyer) I worship (भजन करता हूँ).
He is like a honeybee (मधुमक्खी के समान) enjoying the swelling sweetness (बढ़ती हुई मधुरता) of the flow of nectar (अमृत का प्रवाह) from the cluster of flowers (फूलों का गुच्छा) of the arts of Goddess Parvati (माता पार्वती की कलाएँ), who is the source of all auspiciousness (पूर्ण कल्याणकारी).
Remember the ender of Cupid (कामदेव का अंत करने वाले), the ender of Tripurasura (त्रिपुरासुर का अंत करने वाले), the ender of worldly bonds (भव बंधन का अंत करने वाले), the ender of Daksha's sacrifice (यज्ञ का अंत करने वाले), the ender of Gajasura and Andhakasura (गजासुर और अंधकासुर का अंत करने वाले), that ender of Death (काल के भी काल/Death's ender) I worship (भजन करता हूँ).
Victory to Lord Shiva (भगवान शिव की जय हो), whose fearsome forehead-fire (भयानक माथे की अग्नि) is burning brightly with a blazing 'dhagat-dhagat' sound (धग-धग की आवाज़ के साथ चमकती हुई) amidst the fast movements (तेजी से घूमने वाले) of the terrifying snakes (डरावने सांप).
Lord Shiva is performing his fierce Tandava dance (प्रचंड ताण्डव नृत्य) in a systematic sequence (क्रम के अनुसार) matching the deep, auspicious beats (गंभीर मंगलमय आवाज़) of the mridangam drum sounding 'dhimat-dhimat-dhimat' (धिमित-धिमित की ध्वनि).
When will I worship Lord Sadashiva (सदाशिव की पूजा करूँगा) with an equal mindset (समान बुद्धि/impartial mind), viewing a hard stone and a comfortable bed (पत्थर और बिस्तर), a snake and a pearl necklace (सांप और मोती की माला), a priceless jewel and a dirt clod (बहुमूल्य रत्न और मिट्टी का टुकड़ा), a friend and an enemy (मित्र और शत्रु) as the same.
When will I look at a blade of grass and a lotus-like eye (तण और कमल के समान आँख), a common citizen and an emperor (साधारण प्रजा और राजा) with the same vision (समभाव/equal vision), and when will I worship Lord Sadashiva (सदाशिव का भजन करूँगा)?
When will I live in a cave-like bower (गुफा जैसी कुटिया) near the celestial Ganga river (देवनदी गंगा के किनारे), being free from evil thoughts (बुरी बुद्धि से मुक्त/devoid of wicked thoughts) and always holding my folded hands (हाथ जोड़कर/saluting hands) over my head?
When will I become happy (मैं कब सुखी होऊंगा), being free from unstable desires (चंचल आँखों की इच्छाओं से मुक्त), fixing my meditation on the forehead of Lord Shiva (ललाट पर ध्यान लगाकर), and continuously chanting the mantra 'Shiva' (शिव नाम का जाप करते हुए)?
May the mass of radiance (तेज का समूह/brilliant luster) emanating from the body of Lord Shiva (शिव जी के शरीर से उत्पन्न), which is beautiful and charming (सुंदर और मन को हरने वाला), shower ultimate joy (परम आनंद) upon our minds day and night (रात-दिन).
That divine luster is fragrant with the sweet honey dripping (मीठा शहद टपकना) from the fresh jasmine flowers (चमेली के फूल) woven into the hair-bundles of the celestial damsels (देवांगनाओं के जूड़े).
May the mantra 'Shiva' be victorious across the world (संसार में विजयी हो), which is the ultimate ornament (मुख्य आभूषण/chief ornament), propagating auspiciousness like the fierce submarine fire (भयानक बड़वानल के समान चमक), and silencing the useless talk (व्यर्थ की बकवास) of those desiring the eight great supernatural powers (आठ सिद्धियों की कामना करने वाले).
That mantra is chanted by the beautiful-eyed Goddess Parvati (सुंदर आँखों वाली माता पार्वती) at the auspicious time of marriage (विवाह के मंगल समय पर) and acts as a decoration (गहना/ornament) for the universe.
Indeed, the person who regularly recites (रोज पाठ करता है), remembers (स्मरण करता है), and speaks (बोलता है) this supreme and pure hymn (उत्तम और पवित्र स्तोत्र), constantly achieves ultimate purity (निरंतर परम शुद्धि पाता है).
He quickly attains deep devotion (शीघ्र ही सच्ची भक्ति पाता है) towards the supreme Guru Shiva, and there is no other way (दूसरा कोई रास्ता नहीं), because meditating on Lord Shankara (शिव जी का चिंतन) destroys all illusions of living beings (जीवों का मोह दूर करने वाला है).
The person who recites this hymn composed by the ten-headed Ravana (दशमुख रावण द्वारा गाया हुआ) at the end of evening prayers (प्रदोष काल की पूजा के अंत में) with devotion focused on Lord Shambhu (शिव जी की पूजा में लीन होकर),
To him, Lord Shambhu always grants stable wealth (स्थिर लक्ष्मी/permanent wealth) along with chariots, elephants, and royal horses (रथ, हाथी और उत्तम घोड़ों से युक्त), and the Goddess of Fortune remains ever-graceful (हमेशा प्रसन्न रहने वाली).
Thus ends the Sri Shiva Tandava Stotram (श्री शिव ताण्डव स्तोत्र समाप्त हुआ) composed by King Ravana (रावण द्वारा रचित).
